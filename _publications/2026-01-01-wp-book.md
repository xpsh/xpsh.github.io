This book describes what we know of the Western Plains languages in Taiwan, all of which went extinct in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries. I attempt to reconstruct the phonological systems of these languages based on a historical top-down comparison with Proto-Austronesian, as well as comparisons with what is known about other Formosan languages. Analyses on what the data tells us regarding their histories and classification within the Austronesian family are also provided.
